About the clinic

Recovery, planned around you — not a template.

Demo Physiotherapy is a patient-focused physiotherapy and rehabilitation centre in Garia, Kolkata. Founded and led by a single lead physiotherapist with four dedicated clinicians, we combine the warmth of a neighbourhood clinic with a structured, modern approach to rehabilitation.

Every patient is assessed individually, treated one-on-one, and progressed against clear functional goals — whether that means climbing stairs comfortably, returning to sport, or simply moving through the day with less discomfort.

Our treatment process

A clear path from first visit to full stride.

No guesswork, no conveyor belt — just four deliberate stages, reviewed together with you.

  1. 1

    Initial Consultation

    We listen first — your history, your discomfort, and the activities you want back in your life.

  2. 2

    Assessment

    A hands-on evaluation of mobility, strength, posture and movement patterns to find the root contributors.

  3. 3

    Personalized Treatment

    A plan built for you — manual therapy, guided exercise and practical home routines with clear goals.

  4. 4

    Progress Monitoring

    We re-measure regularly and adjust the plan, so every session moves you toward functional recovery.

Typical first visit: 45–60 minutes (sample)

Ask the clinic

Physiotherapy is a healthcare profession that uses movement, exercise, manual therapy and education to help people manage pain, restore mobility and improve physical function — without surgery or medication as the first line of care.

If pain or stiffness persists beyond a few days, keeps returning, limits daily activities, or follows an injury or surgery, it's worth an assessment. Early guidance often prevents small problems from becoming stubborn ones.

Sessions typically run 30–60 minutes depending on your plan (sample timing). First visits are usually longer because they include a detailed assessment and goal-setting discussion.
